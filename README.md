## AI-Assisted Research Software Development

Alongside my experimental research, I design and lead the development of scientific software using AI-assisted programming. I identify scientific needs, specify workflows, guide implementation, and critically evaluate the results. My goal is not to develop AI models, but to use AI to make experimental research more rigorous, efficient, and accessible.

**Selected projects**

- **AI-Native Lab** — A modular environment intended to provide scientific, computational, technical, and administrative assistance to every laboratory member.
- **Scientific Discovery Engine (SDE)** — An evidence-based framework for organizing observations, challenging hypotheses, and generating testable predictions while keeping scientific decisions with researchers.
- **Microscopy Quantification & Lab Analysis Tools** — Working components for single-cell data summaries, statistical analysis, quality control, reproducible reports, and publication-oriented plots.
- **GatedSprint** — Human-supervised workflows for planning, reviewing, and refining research-related documents and applications.

## Vision: An AI-Native Research Laboratory

My long-term goal is to make advanced research support accessible to every scientist, including students without programming experience. Capabilities that have traditionally required a dedicated computational collaborator, technical specialist, or administrative assistant should become easier for all laboratory members to use.

Rather than requiring researchers to learn and manage many separate tools, **AI-Native Lab aims to identify and connect the right capabilities through a simple interface**, with human oversight of scientific reasoning, sensitive information, and consequential actions.

The purpose is to reduce technical and administrative barriers so researchers can devote more time to designing experiments, interpreting evidence, and making discoveries.
